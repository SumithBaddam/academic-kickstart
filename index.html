Sumith Reddi Baddam

Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon Ads, based in Seattle, WA — building machine learning systems for ad relevance, ranking, and targeting.

I develop and optimize machine learning models to improve advertising performance and customer experience — building scalable algorithms for ad ranking, running rigorous A/B tests, and performing statistical analyses to improve system accuracy. Before this role, I worked on speech recognition for the AI Shopping Agent at Amazon Alexa, and built the ML pipeline behind resource-provisioning estimates for AWS CloudFormation.

I hold master's degrees in Data Science from Indiana University Bloomington and in Information Technology from IIIT Bangalore. Earlier, I spent 2.5 years as a Data Scientist at Cisco Systems, building deep learning models for product quality and engineering productivity, and I've delivered keynote talks at IIM Bangalore and IISc Bangalore.
